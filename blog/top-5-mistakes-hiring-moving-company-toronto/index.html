Top 5 Mistakes to Avoid When Hiring a Moving Company in Toronto

By Moving Papa Team•December 8, 2025•6 min read
Toronto MovingMoving TipsHiring MoversMoving Mistakes

Hiring a moving company should make your life easier, not harder. But unfortunately, not all moving experiences are created equal. In a busy city like Toronto, where traffic, high-rise buildings, and tight schedules come into play, choosing the right movers can make or break your moving day.

At Moving Papa, we've seen firsthand how hiring the wrong company can turn a simple move into a stressful and costly ordeal. Whether you're relocating within the city or moving to the suburbs, avoiding these five common mistakes can help ensure a smooth and successful move.

1. Not Doing Enough Research

One of the most common mistakes people make is hiring the first moving company they find online or choosing based solely on price. Taking a few extra minutes to do your research can make all the difference. Look into at least a few companies, compare services, and check reviews from Toronto customers who have used them before.

At Moving Papa, we're proud of the reputation we've built across the city. Many of our customers find us through word of mouth or come back for a second or third move, which speaks to the trust we've earned. But regardless of who you hire, it's important to dig into real customer experiences and look for consistency in service quality.

Be sure to visit the company's website, confirm they have a physical Toronto address, and check for licensing and insurance. A professional moving company should be easy to reach, clear about what they offer, and open to answering your questions before you commit.

2. Skipping the In-Home or Virtual Estimate

Some people assume that giving a rough idea of the number of boxes or furniture items over the phone is enough to get an accurate quote. The reality is, without seeing your space or inventory, a moving company is only guessing.

Professional movers will usually offer a free in-home or virtual estimate. This helps ensure the quote reflects the actual workload and avoids surprise charges on moving day. If a company refuses to provide an estimate or only offers a vague number, proceed with caution.

In Toronto, factors like stairs, narrow hallways, and condo elevator bookings can affect the complexity of a move. A walkthrough gives movers the chance to prepare for those challenges in advance, so everything runs smoothly on the big day.

3. Failing to Ask About Insurance and Liability Coverage

Let's face it, accidents can happen during any move. That is why it is important to ask about insurance and liability coverage. What happens if something gets broken or lost? What if the truck gets delayed or there is weather-related damage?

A reputable Toronto moving company should offer basic liability coverage and give you the option to purchase additional protection if needed. Make sure you understand the terms and what is included in your quote. Some companies offer low rates but exclude any responsibility for damages, which can leave you out of pocket if something goes wrong.

At Moving Papa, we walk every customer through their coverage options and are happy to answer any questions. We believe that peace of mind should be part of the service.

4. Ignoring Red Flags in Communication

Your first interaction with a moving company can tell you a lot. Are they professional and courteous? Do they respond to emails or phone calls promptly? Are they clear when answering your questions?

Poor communication before the move is often a warning sign of bigger problems to come. If a company seems disorganized, avoids giving clear answers, or is pushy about booking before giving you details, take a step back.

You want a team that is responsive, reliable, and easy to communicate with. Moving day is stressful enough. The last thing you need is to chase your movers for updates or deal with confusion about arrival times or costs.

5. Choosing Price Over Value

Everyone wants to save money when moving, and that is understandable. But choosing the cheapest quote without looking at what is included can backfire. Extremely low prices often mean the company is cutting corners. Maybe they use inexperienced staff, skip insurance, or have hidden fees that pop up later.

Instead of asking, "What is the cheapest option?" ask, "What am I getting for this price?" Consider the level of service, the company's reputation, and how comfortable you feel with their team.

A reliable moving company may cost a little more upfront, but it can save you time, stress, and damage in the long run. That is real value.

Bonus Tip: Book Early When Possible

Toronto's moving industry gets especially busy during peak times, such as summer, the end of the month, and weekends. If you leave booking to the last minute, your options may be limited and prices higher.

Even if you are still finalizing your move date, it is worth reaching out to moving companies early to get a quote and secure your spot. Many movers, including Moving Papa, offer flexible scheduling or rescheduling options if plans change.

Hiring a moving company is more than just renting a truck. It is about trusting someone to handle your belongings and support you during a major life change. By avoiding the most common hiring mistakes you can protect yourself from unnecessary headaches and ensure a much smoother move.
